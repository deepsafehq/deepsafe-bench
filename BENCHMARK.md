# Benchmark Results

Generated by `eval/scripts/generate_benchmark.py` from
`eval/results/predictions_medium.csv.gz`. Every number below is
reproducible from that file: 15,499 samples, 411 generators.

## Headline

- **Overall recall on fakes: 66.2%**
- **False positive rate on real media: 6.5%**

Read that as: roughly a third of fake media passes undetected, and about
one in fifteen genuine files is wrongly flagged. This is the honest
operating point of an ensemble of 19 published detectors.

## Ensemble AUC by modality

| Modality | Samples | AUC |
|---|---|---|
| Image | 10,000 | 0.9466 |
| Audio | 3,500 | 0.8290 |
| Video | 1,999 | 0.6694 |

These are **held-out evaluation** figures. They are lower than the
cross-validated figures produced during meta-learner training, and the
gap is the point: cross-validated scores on the training distribution
overstate what a detector does in the wild.

## Provenance services

These answer *is there a watermark or manifest here*, not *is this fake*.
AUC over the full set is the wrong metric for them and is shown only to
make clear why they are excluded from the ranking above: a watermark
detector scores near chance on media that carries no watermark.

| Service | Samples | AUC |
|---|---|---|
| audioseal | 4,225 | 0.5042 |
| c2pa | 15,499 | 0.5066 |
| sdxl_watermark | 15,499 | 0.5000 |
| videoseal | 15,499 | 0.5000 |

When a signal *is* present, provenance is far stronger evidence than any
detector score. A C2PA manifest is a fact; a model score is an estimate.

## Caveats

- One evaluation run (2026-04-10). Not averaged over seeds.
- TrustMark was added after this run and is absent.
- The manifest carries no `language` field, so no per-language breakdown
  is reported. Do not infer multilingual performance from these numbers.
- Thresholds are the ensemble's own defaults, not tuned per generator.
